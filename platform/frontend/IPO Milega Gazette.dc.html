About & Method
What this is
IPO Milega publishes current, upcoming and recently listed IPOs across NSE, BSE and both SME platforms, drawn from the exchanges. Master data is refreshed daily at 7:30 pm IST; subscription figures follow the exchange tape every five minutes while an issue is open.
Where the numbers come from
Every issue record keeps its source trail: the NSE and BSE listings it was reconciled from, the registrar of record, and the timestamp of the last refresh. Dates marked estimated follow the standard exchange timeline until the exchange reports them.
